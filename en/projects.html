Development
stages

  1. 1

    Brief filling and cost estimation

    You fill out a brief to estimate the project, after which I provide the cost. The agreed price remains unchanged throughout the entire development process.

  2. 2

    Technical specification and contract signing

    A technical specification for the project is created, defining key website functions and stages of work. At this stage, a contract is signed.

  3. 3

    Project scheduling and booking

    We define the start date and project timeline. A 20% prepayment is required to reserve the slot. The remaining balance is paid after completion.

  4. 4

    Design

    A layout and design of the website are created according to requirements. At this stage, functionality and unique features are planned.

  5. 5

    Website development

    First, the desktop version is developed, functionality is implemented, and revisions are made. Then the site is adapted for mobile devices.
