Development
Stages

  1. 1

    Brief filling and cost estimation

    You fill out the project brief for evaluation, after which I provide the price. The agreed price remains unchanged throughout the entire development process.

  2. 2

    Technical specification and contract signing

    The technical specification is created, key website functions and work stages are planned. At this stage, the contract is signed.

  3. 3

    Booking date and project timeline

    We define the start date and project deadline. A 20% prepayment is required to reserve the date. The remaining amount is paid after completion.

  4. 4

    Design

    The layout and design are created according to requirements. Features and unique elements of the future website are planned at this stage.

  5. 5

    Website development

    First, the desktop version is developed, functionality is implemented and adjusted. Then the site is adapted for mobile devices.

  6. 6

    Technical setup

    Technical configuration is performed, search engine integration and other necessary technical processes are completed.

  7. 7

    Completion

    The website goes through final testing, payment is completed, and I deliver the finished project with all access and instructions.
