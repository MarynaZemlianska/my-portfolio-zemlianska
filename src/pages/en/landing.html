stages

  1. 1

    Brief filling and cost estimation

    You fill out a brief to estimate the project, after which I provide the cost. The agreed price remains fixed throughout the development process.

  2. 2

    Technical specification and contract signing

    A technical specification is created, core website functions are defined, and a contract is signed at this stage.

  3. 3

    Project booking and deadlines

    We define the start date and project timeline. A 20% prepayment is required to reserve the slot. The remaining payment is made after completion.

  4. 4

    Design

    A website layout and design are created based on requirements. Features and unique elements are planned at this stage.

  5. 5

    Development

    The desktop version is developed first, functionality is implemented, and revisions are made. Then the mobile version is created.

  6. 6

    Technical setup

    Technical configuration, search engine setup, and other required technical processes are completed.

  7. 7

    Completion

    The site undergoes final testing, payment is completed, and I deliver the finished project with all access and instructions.
