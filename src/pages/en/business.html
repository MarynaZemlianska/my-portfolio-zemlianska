stages

    A layout and design of the website are created according to requirements. At this stage, functionality and unique features are planned.

  5. 5

    First, the desktop version is developed, functionality is implemented, and revisions are made. Then the site is adapted for mobile devices.

  6. 6

    Technical setup

    Technical configuration is carried out: connecting the website to search engines and other necessary technical procedures.

  7. 7

    Completion

    The website goes through a final review, the payment is made, and I hand over the finished project with all access credentials and instructions.
